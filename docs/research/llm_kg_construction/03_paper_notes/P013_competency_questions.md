# P013 — [The Role of Competency Questions in Enterprise Engineering](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-34847-6_3)

**Grüninger and Fox, 1995.**

## Problem

Ontologies are difficult to scope and evaluate when their intended uses are implicit.

## Method

Competency questions characterize what an ontology must represent and answer, guiding both development and evaluation.

## Evidence

This is a foundational ontology-engineering method illustrated through enterprise concepts and formal axioms.

## Limitation

Question quality depends on domain expertise, and competency questions do not by themselves produce source-grounded nodes.

## KnowAct transfer

Freeze questions such as “Can the graph support diagnosis of a stated misconception?” before extraction. Reject concepts and relations that do not serve the declared benchmark scope.
