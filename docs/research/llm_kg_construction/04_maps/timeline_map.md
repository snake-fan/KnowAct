# Timeline Map

- **1995 — Competency questions:** ontology scope is defined by the questions the representation must answer.
- **2003 — Evidence-centered design:** assessment claims are tied to observable evidence and tasks.
- **2022 — Constrained generative extraction:** GenIE demonstrates schema-constrained autoregressive extraction.
- **2024 — Decomposition and verification:** EDC, PiVe, SAC-KG, SPIRES, and GoLLIE make schema, guidance, and verification explicit.
- **2024 — Educational review efficiency:** ACE ranks prerequisite candidates for expert decisions.
- **2025 — Resolution and judging:** KGGen, KARMA, and GraphJudge emphasize clustering, specialized roles, and denoising.
- **2026 — Typed refinement:** GraphRefine moves beyond deletion to error-specific repair.
- **KnowAct direction:** combine these ideas in a fixed, evidence-first authoring protocol and evaluate the protocol itself.
