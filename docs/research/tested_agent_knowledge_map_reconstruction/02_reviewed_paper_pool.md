# Reviewed Top-Conference Paper Pool

## Audit result

Forty-one papers passed the quality gate: 27 direct knowledge-state/CAT papers and 14 supporting ToM, agent, personalization, and active-learning papers. Each title links to an official proceedings page, ACL Anthology, accepted OpenReview page, or publisher DOI.

The `Use` column is a design input, not a claim that the paper's reported gains transfer to KnowAct.

## Count and use discipline

- Direct pool: 27 papers.
- Supporting pool: 14 papers.
- Strict counted total: 41 papers.
- Excluded papers do not contribute to the count.

The design is driven mainly by repeated findings across direct papers: structured state is useful, active selection can reduce interaction cost, graph relations can help but require controls, open-ended evidence carries more signal than correctness alone, and proxy metrics can be misleading. Supporting papers refine agent structure and evaluation safeguards; they do not substitute for direct diagnosis evidence.
