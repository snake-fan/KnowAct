# P006 — [ParLD](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40736)

**Yao et al., AAAI 2026. Reading depth: D1.**

## Contribution

Uses behavior preview, state analysis, performance reasoning, and reflection to update conversational
learning diagnoses across turns, then evaluates prediction and tutoring support.

## KnowAct transfer

Make answer interpretation a typed step and evaluate whether the inferred state predicts held-out
behavior, not only whether its explanation sounds plausible.

## Do not transfer

Multiple role prompts are an architecture choice, not independent evidence of necessity. The full
experiment-table and model-call audit must be completed before citing fine-grained gains.
