# P010 — [PERSONAMEM](https://openreview.net/forum?id=6ox8XZGOqP)

**Jiang et al., COLM 2025. Reading depth: D1.**

## Contribution

Evaluates whether LLMs track evolving user traits and preferences over more than 180 simulated
histories, up to 60 sessions, and 15 task types.

## KnowAct transfer

Add time-indexed or contradictory-profile stress tests and report degradation as history grows.

## Do not transfer

Passive history consumption and response selection do not test active evidence acquisition. The
benchmark's simulator pipeline does not validate KnowAct's simulator.
